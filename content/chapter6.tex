\section{Roadmap}
\subsection{Private and Public Beta Testing}
Stabolut has conducted a private beta testing phase for USB with a limited group of users.
The feedback received during this testing has been crucial in refining the platform and ensuring its robustness.
The company plans to launch a public beta, allowing a wider user base to experience the benefits of USB.\newline
During the public beta phase, Stabolut will continue to monitor and gather feedback from users to further refine the platform and ensure its stability.

\subsection{Launch and Scaling Plans}
Our team is working diligently towards the public launch of USB.
Ahead of the launch, we will be conducting rigorous testing to ensure that the platform is fully functional and ready for use.\newline
In order to scale effectively, we plan to gradually increase the availability of USB on a variety of decentralized exchanges and centralized exchanges, making it easy for users to access and trade the stablecoin.
We will also be exploring partnerships with other players in the industry to increase adoption and awareness of USB.\newline
As demand for USB increases, we will continue to enhance the stability and security of the platform, with the goal of becoming a leading crypto-asset-backed stablecoin in the market.
\subsection{Future Developments and Innovations}
Stabolut has an ongoing commitment to continually improve and innovate USB. Some potential future developments and innovations include:
\begin{itemize}
\item Integration with more decentralized exchanges to increase the robustness of the solution and decrease counterparty risk.
\item Integration with other Layer 2 scaling solutions to improve the transaction speed and lower gas fees.
\item Expand hedging strategies to include additional assets (e.g., ETHUSD) to replicate 2024's 60\% yield performance. 
\item Expansion into other fiat-backed stablecoins, such as euro or yen.
\item Implementation of a reward system for USB holders.
\item Building a user-friendly interface to make it easier for non-technical users to access and use USB.
\item Building APIs and SDKs to make it easier for developers to integrate USB into their applications.
\end{itemize}
Stabolut will continue to monitor the industry and user feedback to identify new opportunities for innovation and improvements.

\subsection{Community Growth Initiatives}  
\begin{itemize}  
\item \textbf{Liquidity Mining}: Earn SBL by providing USB liquidity on DEXs.  
\item \textbf{Governance DAO}: Transition to a decentralized autonomous organization (DAO) governed by SBL holders by 2025.  
\item \textbf{Bug Bounties}: Reward users for identifying vulnerabilities in USB/SBL contracts.  
\end{itemize}
